### 常見縮寫
- **LHS**: Left Hand Side (左側)
- **RHS**: Right Hand Side (右側)
- **ICE**: Internal Combustion Engine (內燃機)
- **MGU-K**: Motor Generator Unit - Kinetic (動能回收馬達)
- **MGU-H**: Motor Generator Unit - Heat (熱能回收馬達)
- **TC**: Turbocharger (渦輪增壓器)
- **ES**: Energy Store (電池)
- **CE**: Control Electronics (控制電子系統)
- **EX**: Exhaust (排氣系統)
- **BBW**: Brake By Wire (線控煞車)
- **PAS**: Power Assisted Steering (動力輔助轉向)
- **DCS**: Driver Cooling System (車手冷卻系統)
- **FOM**: Formula One Management (F1 管理公司)
- **PRV**: Pressure Relief Valve (壓力釋放閥)
